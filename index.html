The Sound

01

Restraint as production

Space is the instrument. Arrangements are built by removal — every element earns its place or it is cut.

02

Low-noise floor

Mastered quiet and wide. QuietWave sits below the loudness war on purpose, rewarding headphones and late hours.

03

Texture over hook

Field recordings, tape wear and room tone carry the melody as often as the synth or the voice does.

02

QuietWave Series

QuietWave is the label's named content vehicle — one sound, four delivery lanes. Each lane has its own release cadence, its own home platform and its own audience.

QW-01SoundCloud · first-listen

QuietWave Sessions

Unmastered passes, alternate takes and works-in-progress posted direct to SoundCloud before catalog delivery.

QW-02Bandcamp · collector

QuietWave Editions

Numbered digital editions with liner notes and stems — the KAFEYANI and ARIA³ collector lane.

QW-03Mixcloud · MDX-III

QuietWave Radio

Monthly long-form DJ transmissions hosted by MDX-III — the rhythm-forward, broadcast side of the imprint.

QW-04DSPs · worldwide

QuietWave Catalog

Finished releases delivered to every major platform through the label's in-house distribution chain.

The Label

Fr. Sandtown-Winchester is an independent digital music label directed by Lee-Wesley Smith III (Northwest / Mason Dixon). The label owns the QuietWave catalog and every artist alias on it, administers its own publishing through EWP/BMI and EPH/ASCAP, and handles registration, delivery and distribution in-house.
